# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, evidence-based, privacy-conscious, and welcoming. Do not publish personal data, discriminatory matching logic, abusive content, or unauthorized third-party material.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or discriminatory behavior are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer contact configured in the repository Security tab. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
