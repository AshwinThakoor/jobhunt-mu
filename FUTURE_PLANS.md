# Future plans

Longer-term opportunities include semantic embeddings for retrieval, learned ranking from consented interaction data, multilingual French/English support, document-template versioning, recruiter integrations, university career-center deployments, and a reusable AI career-services API.

Any future machine-learning system should include:

- documented training-data provenance and consent;
- reproducible preprocessing and versioned artifacts;
- offline evaluation before deployment;
- explainability and human override;
- drift monitoring and rollback;
- privacy impact assessment;
- model and data cards;
- explicit prohibition on protected-attribute discrimination.
